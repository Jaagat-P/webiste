STANFORD / CS224R

Depth-informed
visual navigation

Goal-conditioned trajectory generation

How much does depth help a robot find its way? I compared RGB, estimated depth, and fused observations across imitation learning, offline RL, PPO, and model-based policies on GrandTour and Habitat.

6.2% lower validation loss with RGB + depth versus RGB alone in the imitation-learning experiment.

AUTONOMOUS DRIVING Six ways to plan through uncertainty ↗

STANFORD / CS361 & AA222

Trajectory planning
under uncertainty

A planner and solver comparison on CommonRoad

With Anastasiya Masalava and Ela N. Sigin

I implemented a stochastic chance-constrained planner for autonomous driving. We compared it with mean-prediction and worst-case planning using SQP and CMA-ES to study tradeoffs between risk, driving comfort, convergence, and runtime.

20 driving scenarios, six planner–solver combinations. Evaluated on CommonRoad scenarios drawn from NGSIM traffic recordings.

  • Motion planning
  • Optimization
  • Uncertainty

LANGUAGE MODELING From semantic similarity to Shakespeare ↗

STANFORD / CS224N

Paraphrase detection
& sonnet generation

Adapting GPT-2 with LoRA and DPO

With Kyle Tianshi and Diya Bhattacharjee

We explored efficient fine-tuning and preference optimization for GPT-2 across paraphrase detection, sonnet generation, and sentiment analysis. LoRA performed best on paraphrase detection; DPO highlighted the importance of negative-example quality.

89.1% Quora development accuracy with GPT-2-medium and LoRA, compared with an 82% baseline.

  • Language models
  • LoRA
  • Preference optimization

CS224R · Robot learning

Depth-informed visual navigation

Compared RGB, depth, and fused observations for goal-conditioned robot navigation. RGB + depth reduced validation loss by 6.2% versus RGB alone in the imitation-learning experiment.

CS361 / AA222 · Autonomous driving

Trajectory planning under uncertainty

Implemented a stochastic chance-constrained planner and compared six planner–solver combinations across 20 CommonRoad driving scenarios, measuring tradeoffs in risk, comfort, convergence, and runtime.

CS224N · Language models

Paraphrase detection & sonnet generation

Adapted GPT-2 with LoRA and DPO for paraphrase detection, sonnet generation, and sentiment analysis. GPT-2-medium with LoRA reached 89.1% Quora development accuracy against an 82% baseline.

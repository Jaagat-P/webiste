Research

Applied Intuition · ICLR 2027 (under review)

VLAs Don’t Always Do What They Say: Towards Measuring and Improving Alignment in Self-Driving VLAs

Studied whether self-driving models’ reasoning matches and influences their predicted trajectories. Introduced a multi-axis faithfulness benchmark and TRACR, which uses verified, scene-specific code rewards to improve reasoning–action alignment through reinforcement learning while preserving trajectory accuracy.

Please see my application for the paper.

CS224R & CS199 (ASL)

Depth-Informed Visual Navigation Policies for Goal-Conditioned Trajectory Generation

Compared RGB, depth, and fused observations for goal-conditioned robot navigation. RGB + depth reduced validation loss by 6.2% versus RGB alone in the imitation-learning experiment.

CS361 / AA222 · Autonomous driving

Safe Trajectory Planning Under Uncertainty: A Planner and Solver Comparison on CommonRoad

Implemented a stochastic chance-constrained planner and compared six planner–solver combinations across 20 CommonRoad driving scenarios, measuring tradeoffs in risk, comfort, convergence, and runtime.

Paraphrase Detection and Sonnet Generation with LoRA and DPO

Adapted GPT-2 with LoRA and DPO for paraphrase detection, sonnet generation, and sentiment analysis. GPT-2-medium with LoRA reached 89.1% Quora development accuracy against an 82% baseline.

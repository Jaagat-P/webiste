# STARTS Podcast episode sources

The 49 episode titles, publication dates, durations, Spotify episode links, and original covers were taken from the public STARTS profile on October 6, 2026:
https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/findyourstart3/

`episodes.json` records each source episode page, original cover URL, direct Spotify URL, and corresponding local artwork. Episodes are ordered newest first, following the source profile. Publication dates follow the visible profile labels; exact UTC timestamps are retained in the metadata.

Covers are the original 400 × 400 artwork, converted to WebP without cropping or compositional changes. No guest portraits or episode artwork were generated. All cards link directly to their corresponding Spotify episodes.
